Privacy & AI Processing

How KisanTrack handles farmer, livestock, sensor and camera data.

Data we store

KisanTrack stores account/profile information, livestock records, historical vitals, alerts, inventory records, veterinary logs and uploaded camera captures needed for the features you use.

AI visual screening

Camera images are uploaded to KisanTrack-controlled storage and processed by a server-side AI function. The AI result is a screening aid, not a veterinary diagnosis. Concerning findings should be reviewed by a qualified veterinarian.

AI requests and outcomes are recorded as operational metrics without storing the image in the metrics record.

Camera image retention

Camera captures are configured for a 3-day retention period. Expired capture records and their associated stored images are removed by the scheduled cleanup function.

Retention cleanup requires the Firebase scheduled function to be deployed and Cloud Scheduler to be enabled.

Security & observability

Firestore and Storage access is protected by authenticated, collection-specific rules. Client-side errors may be recorded as short operational telemetry so application failures can be diagnosed. Telemetry is not exposed to other users.

Your data

You can submit an authenticated request for a data export or account deletion. Requests are recorded for controlled processing rather than allowing a browser to directly delete protected records.
