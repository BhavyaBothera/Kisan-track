How KisanTrack Works
कैसे काम करता है

1
Attach Sensors / सेंसर लगाएं
Attach IoT wearable tags to each animal. Sensors track temperature, heart rate, and movement.
2
AI Analyses Data / AI विश्लेषण
Our AI model continuously monitors readings and detects any abnormal health patterns in real time.
3
Get Instant Alerts / अलर्ट पाएं
Receive alerts on your phone the moment an animal needs attention — before illness spreads.

Everything You Need

Camera Monitoring

Live Vitals Dashboard
Real-time graphs of temperature, heart rate, and activity for every animal in your herd.

Animal Profiles
Complete health history, breed info, and 7-day trend charts for each animal.
Sensor Log Upload

Health Reports
Weekly and monthly reports with charts you can export and share with your vet.
